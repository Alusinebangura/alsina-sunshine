Afrobeats Pop 2026

Freetown Nights

A high-energy, late-night dance anthem capturing the pulsating nightlife, ocean breeze, and unmistakable coastal heartbeat of Freetown.

Behind The Track: Inspired by midnight celebrations on Lumley Beach, where neon club lights meet the warm Atlantic surf. Written to celebrate the resilience and joyful nightlife of Sierra Leone's capital. Chorus Hook: "Underneath the Freetown nights, City glowing with neon lights, Feel the bassline shaking the floor, Salone energy, give them more!"

Salone Pride

A soul-stirring and patriotic celebration of Sierra Leonean identity, unity, and resilience, rich with indigenous rhythmic percussion.

Behind The Track: A love letter to Sierra Leone, weaving indigenous talking drums and choir arrangements with uplifting Krio chants championing unity across all districts. Chorus Hook: "Green, white and blue shining bright in the sky, We carry the pride, head held up high! From the hills to the coastal tide, Sing it loud — this is Salone Pride!"

A sun-drenched, upbeat island-infused groove designed to get audiences moving with infectious cadence and joyous vocal charisma.
