About Alsina Sunshine

Sierra Leonean recording artist, songwriter, singer & prolific performer

Alsina Sunshine is a standout Sierra Leonean artist, songwriter, and singer defined by vocal versatility, melodic precision, and high-energy stage performances. Blending the deep emotional warmth of R&B, the razor-sharp cadence of Hip-Hop, and the infectious rhythm of Afrobeats Pop, Alsina produces music at an exacting, professional standard.

From captivating, soul-stirring ballads to festival-ready anthems that light up dance floors across Freetown and beyond, Alsina’s music reflects authentic African storytelling, resilience, joy, and cultural pride.

As a prolific performing singer, Alsina commands the stage with magnetic charisma, dynamic crowd engagement, and an unwavering commitment to elevating Sierra Leonean sound to the global forefront.
